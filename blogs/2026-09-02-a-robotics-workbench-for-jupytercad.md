A unified in-browser workflow from CAD to URDF kinematics Robotics education often hits a wall before the fun even begins. Students and engineers must first navigate a labyrinth of installations: ROS, environment configurations, dependency management, and a patchwork of software, just to visualize a simple robot. It doesn’t have to be this way.
